# ohworks design consistency: plan

Date: 18 Oct 2026. Status: Phase A is done and tested (section 7); Phases B to D are not started. Measures the public site against `ohworks-design-system.md` and the live system (brand book and `tokens.json`), by code audit of `apps/web/src` and `packages/ui/src` and by screenshots of eleven pages at 390px and 1280px.

## 1. What the audit found, in one paragraph

The bones are right. Colour discipline is strong (Tailwind's palette is switched off; two literal colours on the whole site), every grid collapses on phones, nothing scrolls sideways, inputs are 44px with 16px text so iOS never zooms, and the site page follows the ListingPage template almost exactly. The drift is in four places a visitor feels before they could name them: the orange accent, which the system reserves for the one "here" element per view, appears a dozen times on every results page; most buttons, chips and links are 36px against the 44px rule; the same card, notice, heading, field and avatar are built by hand in several slightly different ways, so pages that should feel identical feel like cousins; and a handful of words (Apply, Confirm, my/your/the shortlist, "/ month" versus "a month") change from page to page.

## 2. Decisions

1. **Archivo stays.** The site uses Archivo for display and text, with IBM Plex Mono for data, by a decision recorded in `ohworks-landing-page.md`. The design-system document and the live artifact still say Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans. The system is updated to match the product, with the Archivo type scale (76/48/34/23).
2. **One accent per view wins over the ListingCard note.** The system says both "one survey element per view" and "small accent Enquire" on cards. The stronger rule stands: cards and rows get a secondary "Enquire", and the accent fill is kept for the page's single primary action (Send enquiry on the site page, Find sites on the home hero) and the selected map pin.
3. **44px on touch, 36px allowed with a mouse.** `.oh-btn.sm` and `.oh-chip` keep their 36px look on a pointer device and grow to 44px under `(pointer: coarse)`. Text links that act as controls (Close, Clear all, Remove, Forgot it?) get a 44px hit area everywhere.
4. **Components move up, not sideways.** Where a pattern exists only for owner pages (`PageHead`, `Card`, `Notice` in `components/owners/Frame.tsx` and `Bits.tsx`), it moves into `packages/ui` and the public pages adopt it. Nothing new is invented where the system already names a component.
5. **Tests guard the rules, not the pixels.** Tap-target size, sticky-bar height, text overflow inside buttons, chips and badges, and short screens are asserted. The visual test gets real baselines.

## 4. Order of work

| Phase | Items          | What a visitor gets                                                                              | Days |
| ----- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---- |
| A     | 1, 2, 3, 4, 15 | One accent per view, everything tappable with a thumb, no fixed bars eating the phone, no spill  | 2    |
| B     | 5, 6, 7, 9, 13, 14 | Every page opens the same way, says the same things, and shows the same notice and empty state | 3    |
| C     | 8, 10, 11, 12, 19 | One component per pattern; the dead ones gone; the owner area and public site built from one kit | 5    |
| D     | 16, 17, 18, 20 | Faster on phones, a map that behaves, the system document true again                             | 2    |

Phase A is pure rule enforcement and starts at once. Phases B to D change files across the site, so each lands as its own commit with the test suite green.

## 5. Tests that will guard this

- `responsive.e2e.ts` gains a 360×640 and a 740×360 viewport, and asserts that fixed and sticky bars together stay at or under 25% of the screen height.
- Every visible `a`, `button`, `summary` and `input` is at least 44px tall on the phone projects; `<details>` elements are opened before measuring.
- Text overflow inside `.oh-btn`, `.oh-chip` and `.oh-badge` (scrollWidth over clientWidth) fails the run.
- A page-level count of accent-filled elements (`.oh-btn.accent`, `bg-survey`) must be at most one per view.
- `visual.e2e.ts` gets `toHaveScreenshot` baselines for home, search, a site page and an article, at desktop and Pixel 7, so a drift shows as a diff rather than going unnoticed.
- The owner pages join the responsive run with a signed-in fixture.

## 6. What this does not change

- The Archivo type family and its scale.
- The information architecture, routes, copy voice or any page's content.
- The Payload admin, which was brought to the Day theme separately on 18 Oct 2026 (`ohworks-super-admin-plan.md`, section 12).

## 7. What Phase A changed (18 Oct 2026)

**One accent per view.** The cards' and shortlist rows' "Enquire" and the compare table's per-column "Enquire" are now secondary. The accent stays on the home hero's "Find sites", the site page's "Send enquiry", the shortlist's "Send enquiry to N owners" and the compare page's "Send one enquiry for these". A results or hub page has none: it has no single primary action.

**44px on touch.** Under `(pointer: coarse)`, `.oh-btn.sm`, `.oh-chip`, `.oh-opt`, table-of-contents pills and breadcrumb links are at least 44px tall; a mouse keeps the 36px look (`packages/ui/src/styles/components.css`). Header and footer wordmarks, phone-menu links, footer links, filter "Close", "Clear all" and "Any …" links, compare "Remove", "Forgot it?", the owner bar's links and Sign out, the photo "Remove", "View shortlist", "See all their sites" and the "Email me new sites" summary got a 44px hit area in markup.

**Phone bars.** On a phone the search page shows one strip: Filters, Sort, Map and, once something is saved, "Shortlist N" (`PhoneBar`, kept live by `Basket.tsx`); the saved-sites bar shows from 640px. The filter bar is sticky only from 1024px. The header stops sticking on screens under 480px tall, so a phone on its side keeps the site page's enquire bar without the two covering a third of the screen. The sheets toolbar is sticky from 640px and hides its note on phones. Bottom bars and the filter drawer's footer pad for the iPhone home indicator.

**No spill.** The follow-form and shortlist buttons may wrap, the "Understood as" chip truncates, compare-cell badges wrap, and the owner bar's email and media-kit filenames break anywhere.

**Small text.** Spec and compare source lines are 13px, the fact reference and calendar heads 12px; the share-link input is back to 16px so iOS does not zoom; the charter and for-owners notes are body text, not small print.

**Guard.** `apps/web/e2e/design-rules.e2e.ts` checks twelve pages for: at most one accent; every control 44px tall on the phone project (menus opened first; bot-trap fields and the skip link excepted); fixed and sticky bars at or under 25% of a 360×640 and a 740×360 screen; no label wider than its button, chip or badge. `search.e2e.ts` now expects no accent on results.

**Checked:** design-rules, responsive, search, sites, home and owners on desktop and phone against a freshly built test database: 190 passed, 14 skipped by design (tests for one device only), none failed.

## 8. Where content sits on the page (18 Oct 2026)

The founder found the newsroom's empty state pinned to the left with the right of the page empty, and asked for content to sit in the centre across the site. The rule, now applied everywhere:

| Kind of page | Where its content sits | Pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full width (two or more columns) | The header spans the content area; anything narrower inside it is centred | Home, search, hubs, site page, newsroom front, shortlist, compare, enquiry form and plan, owner area, list your sites |
| Single column (reading or a list) | The whole column is centred, heading included, as articles already were | Charter, how results are ordered, corrections, authors, topics, section indexes, enquiry threads, owner reply, enquiry sent |
| Status page | A centred column with centred text and buttons, like the email-result page | 404, newsroom sign-up confirmation, email results |

`.oh-empty` now centres itself at most 760px wide, so a new empty state is right without help. The search page's "Email me new sites" and "Widen your search" panels follow the same rule. Checked by measuring every page at 1440px for a box or column narrower than the page that hugs its left edge: none left, data-free pages on the development server and populated pages on the sample database. design-rules, responsive, newsroom and home passed on desktop and phone (161 passed).
