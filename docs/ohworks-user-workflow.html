1. The loop on one page

OOHWORKS works when one loop keeps turning: an owner lists a site, the team checks it, a buyer finds it and asks, the owner answers, and the two of them book it between themselves. OOHWORKS never sells and never takes a margin. Its jobs are the listing, the check and the routing. Read across for the order, and down for who acts at each stage. email marks a hand-off made by email.

The one thing that stops the loop today. Every arrow between two people above is an email, and no email service is configured. On this Mac every email is written to the Outbox in /admin instead, so the loop can be walked by hand (section 9). On a public host nobody would hear anything: the owner never gets the code, the enquiry or the "live" notice, and the buyer never gets the reply.

2. Buyer

A small business with a place and a rough budget, an agency with a brief across several sites, or a digital planner new to outdoor advertising. Buyers never need an account. The promise: decide in a minute, ask in one step, and never need to enquire just to learn a basic fact.

From a need to a booked site

Find sites steps 1 to 3 are built in full and tested on sample sites, which now live only in the test database. Nothing real is listed yet.

  1. ArrivesFrom the home search box, a newsroom guide, a link someone shared, or Google.
    / /newsroom/ /in/{city}/
    BuiltGoogle finds nothing yet: samples are never indexed and city pages open at 25 real sites
  2. SearchesTypes a place or a need, then narrows by format, size, price and dates. Every option shows its count. A list beside a map, with "Search this area".
    /search/
    BuiltClaude readingTyped searches are read by Claude only with an Anthropic key
  3. Finds too littleWith three results or fewer the page suggests one change at a time. With none, it offers to email when a site is listed.
    /search/ follow form
    StoredNot sentRequests are saved; no job sends them
  4. Looks closerQuick view from a card, or the full site page: photos, size, exact position, audience with its source, price, availability, the owner and what was checked.
    /quick/{id}/ /in/{city}/{area}/{site}/
    BuiltNearby landmarks and street view wait on a Google Maps key
  5. Saves and comparesSaves up to 50 sites, grouped by owner, with dates and a budget. Compares four side by side. Shares by link or WhatsApp.
    /shortlist/ /compare/
    BuiltKept in one browser; across devices needs a buyer sign-in
  6. Takes it to the clientDownloads the shortlist as Excel or CSV, or prints one site sheet a page.
    /shortlist/sheets/ /api/export/shortlist/
    BuiltSlides and a server-made PDF are not built
  7. EnquiresFor one site, or "Send enquiry to N owners" for the whole shortlist. Dates, brand, artwork, a message and a work email, once. A six-digit code confirms it.
    /enquire/{id}/ /enquire/plan/ /enquire/confirm/
    BuiltEmail keyThe code reaches the buyer only with an email service
  8. Reads the repliesOne page shows every owner asked, who has answered, and each site's availability and quoted rate side by side. Writes back to one owner from there, or simply replies to the email.
    /enquiries/{ref}/?k=
    BuiltEmail keyThe link arrives by email
  9. BooksAgrees the contract, invoice and payment directly with the owner. OOHWORKS is not in the money.
    Outside OOHWORKS
    By designNothing to build; the charter forbids it
  10. Comes backEmails for a saved search, a weekly buyer digest, a shortlist on every device, and an agency workspace with team seats and client folders.
    Follows collection, newsletter, sign-in
    Not builtNeeds scheduled jobs, the email service, and buyer sign-in

3. Owner

An independent owner with 5 to 50 sites, often on a phone; a larger owner with a spreadsheet of hundreds; or an owner with only a PDF media kit. Listing is free, the owner sets the price and how it shows, every enquiry goes straight to them, and nothing is published until they have seen it and the team has checked it.

From a free listing to a booked face

List your sites step 1 is built. Steps 2 (trust and reach) and 3 (the owner's desk) are planned and not started.

  1. Hears of OOHWORKSThe "For owners" page, the "List your sites" button, Google, or the team's outreach and the founding-owner offer.

4. OOHWORKS team

Operations staff who check owners and sites, and editors who run the newsroom. They work in the CMS at /admin.

7. Where the workflow breaks today

Most serious first. "You" means something only you can do; "Code" means work I can do.

  1. No email is sent You

    Every hand-off in section 6 lands in the Outbox. Off this Mac, nobody can sign up, confirm an enquiry or hear a reply.

    Fix: a Resend account with the OOHWORKS domain verified, then RESEND_API_KEY and EMAIL_FROM.

  2. No real sites yet You

    The sample sites and owners now live only in the test database. No page can be indexed and no city page opens until real owners list real sites with photos.

    Fix: the first owners. The founding-owner offer and assisted media-kit entry are the plan's way in.

  3. There is no production site yet You + Code

    Production runs on Google Cloud Run with Cloud SQL in Mumbai, as planned. Netlify has been removed from the project. Vercel still deploys every push to oohworks.vercel.app, where pages that need a database fail.

    Fix: disconnect Vercel, create the Google Cloud projects, and deploy the container with its release step.

  4. Nothing happens on a timer Code

    Saved searches, "tell me when sites are listed", reminders to confirm availability, and the newsletter are stored and never sent.

    Fix: scheduled jobs (Inngest is in the approved stack), once email works.

  5. An owner cannot change a live site Code

    Only price and availability can be changed. A new photo or a corrected size means writing to the team.

    Fix: edits to a live site that go back through review while the live version stays up (List your sites step 2).

  6. Buyers cannot report a listing Code

    The strategy names fake, double-sold and illegal sites as the trust problem in India, and there is no way to flag one. A removed site gives 404, not 410.

    Fix: "Report a listing" on every site page, a queue in the CMS, and the true "gone" answer.

  7. Owners have no public page and no inbox Code

    An owner cannot share one link to all their sites, and enquiries live only in their email.

    Fix: /owners/{name}/ (step 2), then the leads inbox and per-site figures (step 3).

  8. Buyers are remembered by one browser only Code

    A shortlist does not follow an agency planner to a second device, and there are no team seats or client folders.

    Fix: buyer sign-in, then the agency workspace.

  9. Sites only in six cities Code

    Companies can be anywhere, but a site must be in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune or Chennai.

    Fix: open cities as data rather than a fixed list, before the Gulf wave.

8. What I recommend we work on

Close the loop before widening it. A buyer and an owner should be able to complete stages 1 to 9 for real, on one public address, before anything new is added.

First · you, about an hour

Turn on email

Create the Resend account and verify the domain. I wire the key in, send a real code, enquiry and reply end to end, and check what each email says.

Second · code, a few days

Run things on a timer

Saved-search and "first sites listed" emails, a weekly "confirm your availability" nudge to owners, and newsletter confirmation. These keep the loop turning without the team.

Third · code, about a week

Owner step 2

Edits to a live site with a second review, the public owner page, and photos matched to sites by the date and position in each file.

Alongside · code, two days

Report a listing

A report form on every site page, a queue for the team, and the "gone" answer with similar sites for a removed one.

9. Walk the whole loop on this Mac

With the dev server running at localhost:3000. Codes show on the confirm page and every email is in the Outbox.

  1. Owner lists. Open List your sites, fill the form, type the code shown on the next page, add a site in one of the six cities, send it for review.
  2. Team checks. Sign in to /admin, open Sites, filter by "In review", publish it (or return it with a reason and see the owner's page change).
  3. Buyer finds. Open Find sites, search the site's city, open the site page and save it. To try an enquiry across two owners, list a second company the same way first.
  4. Buyer asks. On the shortlist, choose "Send enquiry to N owners", fill it in, and type the code shown.
  5. Owner replies. In /admin › Operations › Outbox, open the email sent to your test owner and follow its reply link. Answer each site.
  6. Buyer reads. In the Outbox, open the buyer's email and follow its link: the replies sit side by side, and you can write back to one owner.
  7. Owner keeps it fresh. Back on the owner's site page, set "booked until" a date, then search again and see the site marked booked.
