On each site, the reviewer

  1. Opens the owner: company, GSTIN, the document
  2. Checks the pin against the road and landmark
  3. Looks at each photograph and its date
  4. Ticks what was checked: owner, location, permit
  5. Publishes it, or returns it with a written reason

What follows by itself

  • Publishing emails the owner that the site is live, with its address
  • Returning it emails the reason, in the reviewer’s words
  • Each tick becomes a badge buyers see
  • A media kit shows as a file on the owner, for the team to enter
OOHWORKS never sells media or takes a media margin. Owners set prices and keep their clients.
