Blog index
Article page
Width

A1 Front page

One lead story, a text list of the latest, then sections with a rail for data and live sites.

Recommended for the index

Best at

Daily news next to slower analysis and guides. The newsletter and the price index get a fixed place. Only the lead needs a photo, so it works from the first week. Folds into a single stream on a phone.

Costs

An editor picks the lead each day. More parts to build than a plain list.

A2 Stream

One column, newest first, every pillar mixed, with filters for section and place.

Best at

Speed and simplicity. Ideal on a phone, easy to paginate, works from the first article.

Costs

No hierarchy. A week of research scrolls away as fast as a two-line news item, and the data has no home.

A3 Magazine grid

One large feature, then a grid of photo cards.

Best at

Showcase and campaign coverage, where the picture is the story.

Costs

Every piece needs a real, credited photo, which our no-stock rule makes expensive early on. Fewer stories per screen, and regulation or pricing news rarely has a picture.

B1 Reading column

One centred column: title, photo, body, sources, author, related.

Best at

News and short pieces. Quickest to build and identical on a phone.

Costs

On a long guide the reader has no map, and the live sites sit far down the page.

B2 Column with rails

Contents on the left, the body in the middle, key facts and live sites on the right.

Recommended for articles

Best at

Guides, analysis and data. The contents, the sourced figures and the sites stay in view while reading. On a phone the rails fold into the column. With the rails switched off for short news it becomes B1, so it is one template.

Costs

More parts to build. Rails look empty on a 300-word item, so they hide below a set length.

B3 Immersive lead

A full-width photo, the title on a card over it, wide pictures through the body.

Best at

Showcase pieces and flagship features.

Costs

Needs strong real photography every time. The first paragraph starts below the fold, and the photo becomes the slowest thing to load. Better as an option on B2 than as the default.
